Why Legacy Bureau Scoring Fails 64 Million Modern Earners

Conventional credit bureaus were built in 1989 for corporate 9-to-5 employees with bi-weekly paystubs. Today's dynamic gig economy requires a modern cashflow underwriting paradigm.

✕

Traditional Bureau (FICO / Vantage)

Backward-Looking Debt Bias
  • Penalizes Debt Avoidance: If you never took high-interest credit cards or loans, you are labeled "unscorable" or given a thin-file subprime score.
  • Fails Variable Inflows: Treats any freelance income fluctuation as an automatic default warning, ignoring net monthly profit margins.
  • Punishes Rate Shopping: Imposes hard inquiry penalties (knocking 5 to 10 points off your score) every time you seek credit.
  • Black Box Rejections: Provides vague adverse action letters with zero actionable mathematical guidance on how to fix your limits.
✓

CredPulse Underwriting Engine

Forward-Looking Cashflow Velocity
  • Rewards Positive Inflow: Evaluates actual cash entering your accounts over 30/60/90 days, approving prime limits without credit file prerequisites.
  • Volatility-Damped Floor: Calculates the 95th percentile worst-case earnings floor so safe credit limits remain secure even during dry spells.
  • Zero Score Harm: Evaluates open banking read-only cash telemetry with absolutely zero hard credit inquiries or score deductions.
  • Transparent Math Rationale: Tells you exactly what your cashflow supports, why your limit is sized at $X,000, and how to expand capacity.

How the CredPulse Underwriting Model Works

Our scoring framework replaces arbitrary black-box algorithms with transparent, mathematically deterministic risk formulas.

01

Cashflow Velocity (30% Weight)

Measures the volume and consistency of net funds entering versus exiting the account over 30, 60, and 90-day intervals. Ratios above 1.2 indicate organic debt-servicing capacity.

Velocity = Verified Inflows / Total Monthly Disbursements
02

Volatility Floor (25% Weight)

Calculates monthly income coefficient of variation to size a conservative stressed baseline earning floor, protecting borrowers and lenders during low-earning months.

Floor = Monthly_Income * (1 - Volatility_Rate)
03

Risk-Adjusted Margin (25% Weight)

Traditional DTI ignores non-discretionary survival costs. RACM isolates essential living expenses (rent, food, utilities) to ensure organic surplus remains before extending credit.

RACM = Income_floor - Living_Costs - Debt_Payments
04

Liquidity Runway (20% Weight)

Measures how many months the applicant can survive with zero income while continuing to service debt commitments. A larger liquid buffer improves resilience to disruptions.

Runway = Liquid Savings / (Living_Costs + Debt_Payments)

This project is submitted to Coppell Hackathon 1 in the Credit category (spanning alternative credit underwriting, cashflow scoring, and personal debt elimination).

Does checking my score on CredPulse hurt my credit?

No. Traditional hard inquiries dock 5 to 10 points from your bureau score. CredPulse evaluates read-only cashflow telemetry with zero inquiry penalties.

Is CredPulse free and open source?

Yes. CredPulse is 100% free and open-source under the MIT license, built as a transparent, client-side public financial tool that never stores or sells your personal financial data.

Why is this better than traditional FICO?

Traditional FICO only looks backward at debt history. CredPulse evaluates real-time cashflow velocity, income stability, and liquid savings, opening fair credit access to thin-file and gig workers.
